# Arcanova — Vision

This is the **north-star vision**: the full dream of what Arcanova could become.

It is intentionally bigger than what we are building right now. The achievable,
ordered path toward this vision lives in `ROADMAP.md`. Think of it this way:

- **VISION.md** (this file) — the destination. Big, ambitious, allowed to be huge.
- **ROADMAP.md** — the path. Small, ordered, achievable steps that actually ship.

A roadmap that stays simple is doing its job. The vision is where ambition lives;
the roadmap is where it gets built, one room at a time.

---

## Core idea

A modern, competitive, social sandbox platform where kids and teens build, play,
compete, learn and create — in a safe, well-designed ecosystem.

Tagline: **Create. Compete. Conquer.**

---

## 1. Competition

Not just games — leagues, seasons and ranking.

- Ranked tiers (e.g. Bronze → Diamond → Champion)
- Match formats: 1v1, 2v2, 5v5, battle royale, parkour races, speedruns
- Season passes with unique cosmetic skins
- Live tournaments
- "Creator vs Players" events

## 2. Creator engine & tools

Powerful creation, but easy to start with.

- Drag-and-drop building
- Lua / Python scripting for older / advanced users
- AI-assisted level design ("make a futuristic arena" → draft generated)
- Real-time collaborative building
- A physics system supporting racing, parkour, shooters, survival, etc.

## 3. Skill-progression system

Learn while playing — educational without feeling like school.

- Skill trees: game design, 3D modelling, coding, strategy, teamplay,
  economy & entrepreneurship
- Players unlock abilities as they play and create

## 4. Game types

- **Arena games** — tactical 5v5 (non-violent, Fortnite-style), capture-the-flag,
  parkour racing, obstacle-course challenges
- **Open worlds** — survival worlds, build-your-own-city, mythology universes
  (e.g. Norse myth: Nøkken, Huldra, jotner)
- **Creator games** — player-made games, voting, weekly featured creators

## 5. Safety — "Safe-Core"

- AI-moderated chat
- Age-appropriate feature sets (e.g. 9–12 vs 13–16)
- Parent dashboard
- Zero-toxicity policy
- Voice chat limited to friends / teams only

## 6. Creator economy

- Players earn coins for playing, ranking and events
- Creators earn coins when their games are played
- Coins spend on skins, effects, building materials
- Older creators may convert coins to real money (with guardian consent)

## 7. Social — "The Hub"

- Hangout areas: skate park, dance zone, mini-games
- Live events and concerts
- Influencer-made worlds

## 8. Standout features

- **AI companions** — a personal AI buddy that learns your style, gives tips in
  competitions, and helps you build
- **Dynamic worlds** — worlds that change based on player actions (climate,
  economy, enemies, resources)
- **Esports league** — a real, youth-friendly league; schools and communities
  could take part; finals could be streamed via media partners

---

## Reality notes — read before roadmapping any of this

Some items here are not just *hard to build* — they carry real-world legal and
safety weight, and must not be treated as ordinary roadmap features:

- **Real-money payouts to minors** — this is regulated financial activity (tax,
  anti-money-laundering, identity verification). It needs legal counsel before
  it is even safe to design.
- **Voice chat + chat moderation for children** — among the most
  responsibility-heavy things on the internet. Needs real trust-&-safety
  expertise and child-safety legal review.
- **A custom game engine with physics** — on its own a multi-year effort for a
  dedicated team.

None of this is "no". It is "this belongs in the vision, and when it becomes
real it needs real experts — a lawyer, child-safety specialists — not just code."

The way to reach this vision is to ship the roadmap, one room at a time.
